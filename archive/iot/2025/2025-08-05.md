【文章标题】：SonicWall firewalls targeted in ransomware attacks, possibly via zero-day
【文章摘要】：SonicWall firewalls have been targeted by ransomware attacks using the Akira ransomware, potentially exploiting a zero-day vulnerability. Organizations are advised to disable SSL VPN services until a patch is available.
